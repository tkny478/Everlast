Automation & Systems Integration

Intelligent Weighbridge Software & Automation Solutions

Eliminate human error, prevent weight fraud, and digitize your fleet operations with Everlast’s automated weighbridge management software.

Our proprietary software links directly with your scale indicators, automated barrier gates, and camera systems to capture gross, tare, and net weights automatically. It seamlessly exports real-time data to ERP systems like SAP, QuickBooks, or custom databases.

📸 ANPR License Plate Integration

Automatic vehicle camera identification reduces processing time and prevents manual driver plate entry errors.

🛡️ Fraud Prevention & Anti-Tamper

Integrated photo capture at weighment time, positioning sensors, and weight variance alerts stop payload manipulation.

🔄 ERP & Accounting Sync

Direct API integration with QuickBooks, SAP, and SQL databases for instant billing and inventory management.

📑 Automated Weighbill Generation

Instant printing and digital PDF delivery of official weight tickets with customizable field data.
