About Us

Delivering high-accuracy weighing solutions, weighbridge installations, and metrology services to East Africa's leading industries.

Who We Are

Everlast Scales & Electronic Services Ltd is a leading Kenyan engineering firm specializing in industrial, commercial, and laboratory weighing systems.

Headquartered in Nairobi, we provide end-to-end metrology solutions—from civil works for multi-ton pitless weighbridges to micro-gram precision balances used in medical and research laboratories.

Our certified technicians work closely with the Weights & Measures Department (Legal Metrology) to ensure that every scale we supply, repair, or service maintains legal compliance and operational integrity.

"Quality Weighing Solutions for a Fair Life."
